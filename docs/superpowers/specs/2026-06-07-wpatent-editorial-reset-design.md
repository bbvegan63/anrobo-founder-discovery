# W&Patent Editorial Reset Design

> **Date:** 2026-06-07
> **Scope:** Reset W&Patent around a rewritten pillar plus a three-post weekly series, with a sharper Andrew-led house voice and a bare-minimum public surface
> **Status:** Approved design

## Purpose

This spec defines a fresh-start editorial reset for W&Patent.

The earlier W&Patent work produced useful infrastructure:

- founder-discovery tracking
- an Andrew-led advisory reposition
- a soft-minimal site shell
- prompt-evidence measurement
- a first support-page publication workflow

But the active public story still feels fragmented.
The user wants a cleaner restart built directly from the draft bundle in `drafts/wpatent/`, with:

- a stronger editorial point of view
- a simpler top-level site
- weekly publishing discipline
- AI discovery as the primary operating goal

This design therefore changes the center of gravity again:

- the draft pillar becomes the new flagship strategy page
- the three draft posts become the first weekly series
- the sharper editorial voice becomes the new W&Patent house voice
- older secondary pages are retired or redirected instead of lingering as competing narratives

## Core Decision

W&Patent should be rebuilt around this message:

**Andrew Leung brings a patent agent entrepreneur perspective and articulates patent strategy in founder language.**

That message replaces the calmer Phase 2 “small advisory shell plus support layer” framing as the primary public story.

The new editorial reset should:

- keep Andrew Leung as the visible voice
- keep W&Patent as the branded container
- use startup patent strategy as the flagship topic
- use the draft pillar and posts as the editorial backbone
- keep virtual marking as a real service, but secondary to the strategy narrative
- keep OpenFor member context as proof of ecosystem relevance, not the homepage headline

## New House Voice

The new W&Patent voice should be sharper than the current live pages, but it should not collapse into grievance or sensationalism.

The voice target is:

- candid
- founder-translated
- corrective
- opinionated
- practical

It should sound like Andrew telling founders what matters and what common advice gets wrong.

It should not sound like:

- generic legal marketing
- neutral enterprise copy
- marketplace/product brochure language
- unsupported culture-war rhetoric

The safest tonal model is:

- sharper than the current strategy page
- less combative than “the system is corrupt”
- centered on Andrew’s ability to translate patent strategy into founder language

## Editorial Architecture

The new editorial backbone should be:

1. one rewritten pillar
2. three rewritten support posts
3. one post published each week after the pillar launch

### Pillar

The draft pillar should be rewritten into the new flagship strategy page.

It should replace the current strategic role of:

- `https://wpatent.com/startup-patent-strategy.htm`

The URL should stay the same.
The content should change completely.

Reason:

- preserve continuity on the strongest existing strategy URL
- keep a stable canonical page for discovery
- avoid replacing the anchor with a brand-new slug unnecessarily

### Post 1

`provisional-vs-nda` should be rewritten from scratch and republished as the first weekly support post.

Its job is:

- correct founder confusion about confidentiality versus optionality
- reinforce Andrew’s founder-language translation value
- support the new pillar directly

Preferred live URL:

- keep `https://wpatent.com/provisional-vs-nda.htm`

Reason:

- the slug already exists live
- it is already aligned with the new cluster
- rewriting in place is cleaner than creating a second near-duplicate URL

### Post 2

`provisional-and-poc-budget` should be rewritten from scratch and published in the second weekly slot.

Its job is:

- explain timing and budget tradeoffs in founder language
- connect patent work to limited startup capital decisions
- reinforce the new pillar’s worldview

Preferred live URL:

- `https://wpatent.com/provisional-and-poc-budget.htm`

### Post 3

`draw-first-write-second` should be rewritten from scratch and published in the third weekly slot.

Its job is:

- explain possession and drawings in founder language
- correct the common misconception that provisional quality is mainly about dense prose
- deepen Andrew’s authority on drafting judgment

Preferred live URL:

- `https://wpatent.com/draw-first-write-second.htm`

## Pillar Role

The pillar should become the new main strategy page, not merely a separate article underneath the current strategy page.

That means:

- it replaces the current content of `startup-patent-strategy.htm`
- it becomes the page linked from the homepage as the first thing founders should read
- it becomes the main strategy page used in later LinkedIn and ecosystem signal work

The pillar should remain founder-useful, but it should also be willing to say:

- what advice founders hear too often
- what matters more than the usual script
- what Andrew would tell a founder with limited time and budget

## Site Surface

The top-level public site should stay bare minimum.

Keep only:

1. `Home`
2. `Strategy`
3. `Services`
4. `About`
5. weekly post pages as they publish

The goal is not to keep a large support layer under the hood.
The goal is to let the new pillar-led system define the brand.

### Home

The homepage should:

- introduce Andrew using the new translation framing
- point users to the rewritten pillar first
- use direct, founder-facing language
- avoid too many concepts at once

### Strategy

The strategy slot should be the rewritten pillar at:

- `startup-patent-strategy.htm`

### Services

The services page should stay live, but should be rewritten to match the new house voice.

The service set should stay small:

- founder patent advisory
- virtual marking
- limited founder decision support

### About

The about page should be rewritten around:

- Andrew Leung
- patent agent entrepreneur framing
- patent strategy translated into founder language
- OpenFor member context

## Retirement And Redirect Rule

Secondary pages from the older mixed strategy should be retired or redirected if they do not serve the new pillar-led editorial system.

This is acceptable for AI discovery only if each retirement has a clear destination.

### Keep live

- `index.html`
- `startup-patent-strategy.htm`
- `services.htm`
- `about.htm`
- `provisional-vs-nda.htm`
- the two additional weekly-post URLs as they launch

### Retire or redirect

Pages that belong to the earlier mixed strategy should be candidates for retirement or redirect, especially if they dilute the new story.

Strong candidates include:

- `patent-strategy-open-licensing.htm`
- `trust-chain.htm`
- `trust-chain-explainer.htm`
- older support notes whose main value will be absorbed into the new pillar or weekly series

### Redirect principle

Use redirects or retired-helper treatment rather than hard deletion.

Default destinations:

- strategy/topic pages -> `startup-patent-strategy.htm`
- founder-proof pages -> `about.htm`
- service pages -> `services.htm`

## Weekly Publishing Cadence

The weekly cadence should be:

- week 0: launch rewritten pillar
- week 1: launch rewritten `provisional-vs-nda`
- week 2: launch rewritten `provisional-and-poc-budget`
- week 3: launch rewritten `draw-first-write-second`

This is a restart, not an acknowledgment of the earlier support-page sequence.

Operationally:

- the earlier `provisional-vs-nda` publication is treated as pre-reset history
- the rewritten version becomes the new week-1 version of the reset series

## External Signal Sequence

External signals should reinforce the pillar-led restart sequentially, not all at once.

Recommended order:

1. after the new pillar goes live, publish Andrew’s first LinkedIn post pointing only to `startup-patent-strategy.htm`
2. after post 1 goes live, publish a second LinkedIn reinforcement around that post
3. after the new pillar and at least one support post are live, send the OpenFor member-recognition ask
4. continue publishing and measuring one week at a time

This makes the editorial reset the main signal source and uses LinkedIn/OpenFor as reinforcement rather than as substitutes for the site story.

## AI Discovery Interpretation

The expected discovery benefit of this reset is not “more pages means more score.”

The expected benefit is:

- fewer competing narratives
- one stronger canonical strategy page
- clearer Andrew + W&Patent + founder-strategy pattern
- repeated founder-language articulation across a coherent series

Success should be judged by whether answer systems increasingly reflect:

- `Andrew Leung`
- `W&Patent`
- startup patent strategy
- founder-language framing
- eventually OpenFor-linked understanding

This design intentionally accepts some loss of old support-page surface area in exchange for stronger editorial coherence.

## Guardrails

Because the draft bundle is sharper than the current live site, the rewrite should enforce a few guardrails:

- preserve the sharpness, but remove brittle or overclaiming lines unless they can be defended
- prefer “what founders usually get told wrong” over unsupported accusations
- keep practical next-step usefulness in every article
- keep the site legally and reputationally credible even when it is blunt

In particular, claims like competitors filing from public GitHub in 45 minutes should be softened or framed as a real risk pattern unless direct support is available.

## Non-Goals

This reset does not aim to:

- keep the old mixed strategy alive in parallel
- preserve every previous support page as a public destination
- maintain the calmer advisory-only tone
- expand to more incubators or service lines immediately

Those later expansions remain possible, but they are not the starting point of this reset.

## Recommendation

The recommended move is a full editorial reset:

- rewrite the pillar into the new flagship strategy page
- rewrite the three posts from scratch
- publish one per week
- rewrite the rest of the site around the new sharper Andrew-led voice
- retire or redirect secondary pages that belong to the older story

This gives W&Patent a genuine fresh start while keeping the domain, founder, and strategy anchor coherent enough to support AI discovery.
