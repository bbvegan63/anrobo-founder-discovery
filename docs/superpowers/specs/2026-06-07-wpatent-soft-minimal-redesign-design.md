# W&Patent Soft-Minimal Redesign Design

> **Date:** 2026-06-07
> **Scope:** Simplify the top-level W&Patent visitor experience while preserving AI-discovery support
> **Status:** Approved design

## Decision

Apply a soft-minimal redesign to `wpatent.com`.

The site should become:

- simpler for human visitors
- more obviously Andrew-led
- easier to understand in one pass

without sacrificing:

- `Andrew Leung` naming
- `W&Patent` entity grounding
- structured discovery support
- strong support pages that can still help citation and answer-engine retrieval

This is not a full content purge.
It is a simplification of the primary user path.

## Why This Exists

The current advisory-first reposition improved the business story, but the top-level site can still feel too concept-heavy and too busy for a first-time visitor.

The main confusion risk is that the site still asks visitors to parse too many layers at once:

- Andrew as advisor
- W&Patent as brand
- strategy guidance
- services
- OpenFor context
- Trust Chain
- commercialization framing
- secondary proof pages

That may be defensible as a knowledge system, but it is not the clearest visitor experience.

The redesign should reduce cognitive load while preserving the semantic and citation surfaces that help AI discovery.

## Positioning Split

Use this explicit split:

- `Andrew Leung` is the visible advisory voice
- `W&Patent` remains the branded container and entity shell

Human-facing clarity should become Andrew-led.
Semantic clarity should continue to expose W&Patent strongly.

That means:

- Andrew leads the headline and service framing
- W&Patent stays in titles, schema, canonicals, footer, and internal links

Do not turn the site into `Andrew only`.
The safer model is:

`Andrew is the voice. W&Patent is the branded container.`

## Primary Visitor Path

Reduce the top-level visitor experience to four primary surfaces:

1. `Home`
2. `Strategy`
3. `Services`
4. `About`

These are the only pages that should feel primary in the main navigation and top-level CTA flow.

### Home

Purpose:

- explain who Andrew is
- explain what he helps with
- present one clear next reading path
- present one clear services path

### Strategy

Primary page:

- `startup-patent-strategy.htm`

Purpose:

- act as the flagship authority page
- answer the founder question quickly
- function as the main “read this first” surface

### Services

Purpose:

- explain the real offer simply
- reduce service sprawl
- support contact intent

### About

Purpose:

- provide short founder proof
- tie Andrew, W&Patent, and OpenFor together clearly

## Bare-Minimum Service Framing

The main service experience should reduce to:

1. startup patent strategy advisory
2. virtual marking

Optional third item:

- founder decision support / founder articulation support

Do not present a broad service catalog.
Do not keep commercialization as a top-level conversion route in this redesign.

Commercialization can remain available in the support layer, but it should not dominate the simplified top-level experience.

## Demotion Rules

These should be removed from the primary visitor path:

- `FAQ` in top-level nav
- `Trust Chain` in top-level nav
- commercialization as a homepage pillar
- excessive card grids or framework explanation on the homepage
- extra CTA rows that compete with the main action

These can remain live, but should be demoted from the main path:

- `faq.htm`
- `trust-chain.htm`
- `trust-chain-explainer.htm`
- `patent-commercialization-for-founders.htm`
- `patent-strategy-open-licensing.htm`
- `andrew-leung-startup-patent-strategy.htm`

Support pages should still be reachable through:

- internal links
- sitemap
- secondary support references

They do not need to remain in the first-click visitor flow.

## What Must Stay For AI Discovery

The redesign should simplify the experience layer, not strip out the semantic layer.

Keep explicitly:

- page titles that still expose `Andrew Leung` and `W&Patent` where appropriate
- canonical URLs
- `Organization`, `Person`, and `Article` schema where already useful
- internal links among the strongest core and support pages
- sitemap entries for strong citation surfaces
- footer entity wording

The support layer should remain rich enough that answer engines can still connect:

- `Andrew Leung`
- `W&Patent`
- founder patent strategy
- OpenFor member
- virtual marking

## Page Simplification Direction

### Homepage

Simplify aggressively.

Keep:

- one short statement on who Andrew is
- one short statement on what he helps founders do
- one CTA to `startup-patent-strategy.htm`
- one CTA to `services.htm`
- a short factual OpenFor mention

Reduce:

- explanatory card count
- concept count
- parallel topic routes
- long theory framing

The homepage should answer:

- who is Andrew?
- what does he help with?
- what should I read first?
- how do I contact him?

### Strategy Page

Keep as the primary authority page.

Tighten:

- the top half
- speed to answer
- scannability

Preserve:

- direct-answer blocks
- founder framing
- internal support links

### Services Page

Shorten into a simpler offer page.

Keep:

- startup patent strategy advisory
- virtual marking
- optional short founder decision-support framing

Reduce:

- service breadth
- commercialization-heavy explanation
- anything that reads like a long catalog

### About Page

Shorten into a cleaner founder-proof page.

Preserve:

- Andrew Leung
- patent agent turned entrepreneur
- OpenFor member
- W&Patent relationship

Reduce:

- secondary explanation that does not help trust or next-step clarity

## Tone

Use a tone that is:

- direct
- calm
- practical
- founder-facing
- intuitive on first read

Avoid:

- too many conceptual layers at once
- heavy explanatory scaffolding on the homepage
- making visitors learn the full framework before understanding the offer

## Non-Goals

This redesign is not for:

- deleting strong support pages outright
- removing W&Patent from the semantic layer
- turning the site into a personal blog
- replacing the flagship strategy page with a new anchor
- introducing a larger service menu

## Recommendation

The recommended move is a `soft-minimal redesign`:

- simplify the top-level visitor path aggressively
- preserve the AI-discovery support layer underneath
- center the human experience on Andrew advisory
- keep W&Patent as the semantic and branded shell

This is the best balance between:

- visitor intuitiveness
- founder trust
- and continued discovery support
