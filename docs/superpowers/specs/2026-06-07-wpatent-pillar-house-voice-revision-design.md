# W&Patent Pillar House Voice Revision Design

> **Date:** 2026-06-07
> **Scope:** Revise the W&Patent flagship pillar so the new house voice feels sharper, cleaner, and more founder-native without overclaiming
> **Status:** Approved design

## Purpose

This spec defines a narrow editorial revision to the new W&Patent pillar.

The week-0 editorial reset is already live.
The strategic structure is not changing.
The pillar still needs a stronger house voice so it reads less like a drafted article and more like a distinctive Andrew-led founder brief.

The revision should improve:

- tone
- cadence
- compression
- section differentiation
- trustworthiness under sharper language

It should not reopen the broader editorial-reset architecture.

## Current Source Of Truth

The voice revision applies to both:

- `outputs/publishing/2026-06-07-pillar-startup-patent-strategy.md`
- `https://wpatent.com/startup-patent-strategy.htm`

The Markdown asset remains the source-of-truth draft.
The live HTML page should stay aligned with that source after the revision.

## Core Goal

The revised pillar should sound like:

- Andrew Leung speaking with judgment
- a founder brief rather than a legal article
- sharper than the earlier calm-voice strategy page
- still measured enough to support trust and AI discovery

The target balance is:

- part manifesto
- part founder operating brief
- without drifting into accusation, grievance, or unsupported system claims

## Voice Rules

The rewrite should follow these rules:

1. **One confident voice**
The piece should sound like one person making a coherent case, not like stitched-together notes.

2. **Founder language first**
Prefer plain, practical language over legal-performance language.

3. **Sharper, not louder**
Keep the edge, but make it come from contrast, specificity, and clarity rather than inflated rhetoric.

4. **Measured claims**
Frame strong lines as:
- Andrew’s judgment
- common founder pattern
- practical risk

Avoid sounding like every claim is universal law.

5. **Less repeated framing**
Do not keep restating:
- founders hear a script
- Andrew’s view is
- founder language
- leverage / optionality

Those ideas should remain present, but with more variation and tighter pacing.

6. **Cleaner declarative rhythm**
Use shorter, more decisive sentences where possible.
Let a few hard lines land instead of crowding every paragraph with emphasis.

## Rewrite Pattern

The structure should stay the same:

- opening setup
- `Opening Thesis`
- `Provisional Before NDA`
- `Draw First. Write Second.`
- `Build And File In Parallel`
- `The Unifying Founder Strategy`
- `One-Paragraph Summary`
- `Warning & Disclaimer`

Within that structure, each section should follow a consistent pattern:

1. what founders are usually told
2. why that framing is incomplete
3. what matters more in practice

This keeps the article coherent without making every section sound interchangeable.

## Specific Revision Targets

### Headline And Lead

Keep the current sharp title.

Revise the lead so it feels more immediate and more founder-problem-first.
The title currently promises a sharper reveal than the opening fully delivers.
The first paragraphs should more quickly establish:

- founders hear a standard script
- the script is incomplete
- this guide is the corrective

### Opening Section

The opening should feel more like an argument and less like an introduction to an argument.

It should:

- establish the mismatch between founder constraints and standard patent process
- name the hidden tradeoffs cleanly
- move quickly into what founders should care about

### Core Sections

Each section should carry its own energy:

- `Provisional Before NDA` should feel like a false-comfort correction
- `Draw First. Write Second.` should feel like possession and evidence logic
- `Build And File In Parallel` should feel like timing pressure and shrinking window

### Table Section

Keep the comparison table.
Tighten it so it feels like the compression point of the article rather than just a recap.

### Ending

Shorten and sharpen the close.
The article should end with a founder operating rule, not a softened restatement of the intro.

### Disclaimer

Keep the disclaimer plain and low-drama.
It should not dilute the article’s confidence, but it should remain clearly compliant and non-performative.

## Guardrails

The revision should preserve the editorial-reset guardrails already approved elsewhere.

That means:

- no unsupported institutional-intent claims
- no sensational examples unless softened or directly supportable
- no escalation into “the system is corrupt” language
- no sharpness for its own sake

One helpful test:

If a sentence sounds more written to provoke than to clarify, rewrite it.

## Out Of Scope

This voice revision does not:

- change the weekly sequence
- change post order
- revive retired support pages
- reopen the OpenFor or LinkedIn signal strategy
- introduce new core claims beyond the existing pillar logic

## Recommendation

Use a full line-by-line voice rewrite while keeping the current backbone intact.

That is the best balance because it:

- improves the entire article evenly
- preserves the approved structure
- keeps the sharper editorial identity
- lowers repetition
- avoids accidental strategy drift

The intended end state is:

**more distinctive, more quotable, more founder-native, and still defensible enough to support trust and discovery.**
