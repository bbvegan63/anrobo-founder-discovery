# W&Patent Editorial Reset Baseline

## New Story

W&Patent is being reset around one sharper pillar and one weekly founder-language post at a time.

## Starting Read

The reset starts from:

- a live domain with a good canonical strategy URL

## Why The Reset Exists

The earlier mixed strategy created too many competing narratives.
The reset exists to make one strategy page and one post series the main thing answer systems learn.

## Rule For Weekly Publishing

The new weekly series is measured as a new editorial sequence.
No later week should be judged as simple continuation of the pre-reset calm-voice rollout.
