# OpenFor.co Progress Report

> **Date:** 2026-06-06
> **Report:** Baseline (first owner-facing report)
> **Working total:** 60/100

## Executive Summary

OpenFor.co is a founder-led editorial and incubator surface with strong founder identity and regional context. It currently outperforms W&Patent on branded retrieval — answer engines can find openfor.co when prompted with site-specific cues — but broad discovery remains weak. The site works best for commentary and ecosystem interpretation rather than answer-shaped content. The highest-leverage improvements would add explicit founder-view summary blocks and strengthen the Erdinc Ekinci to OpenFor.co to topic viewpoint chain on key pages.

## What Changed Since Last Report

This is the first baseline report, so there is no prior owner-facing report to compare against. The scorecard snapshot dates to May 19, 2026, and reflects the initial measurement round.

## Evidence Highlights

- **Branded retrieval works:** All three identity retrieval prompts cited `openfor.co`, and one also named `Erdinc Ekinci` — stronger branded grounding than W&Patent's current state
- **Broad discovery is a hard area:** Zero citations across three broad comparison prompts — the site is not surfaced for non-branded queries in its topic space
- **No external signals logged:** No offsite distribution, founder posts, or response signals are recorded yet in this workspace

## Current Blockers

1. **Weak broad discovery** — 2/20 with zero citations; the site is invisible to AI systems answering general questions about solo founder, AI, or Tokyo startup topics
2. **No answer-shaped content** — the site is opinion-forward and event-led; answer engines prefer pages that directly answer implied questions
3. **Partial branded naming** — while the site is cited, the founder (`Erdinc Ekinci`) is only named in 1/3 branded prompts, leaving entity recognition incomplete
4. **No external proof signals** — no offsite citations, guest content, or distribution signals reinforcing the site or founder name

## Next Recommended Moves

1. **Add direct founder-view summary blocks** to key pages — short TL;DR or Q&A sections that answer the site's implied thesis clearly for AI consumption
2. **Strengthen the Erdinc Ekinci -> OpenFor.co -> topic viewpoint chain** on pages most likely to be surfaced by answer engines
3. **Lean into commentary and regional ecosystem intent** rather than generic how-to positioning — the site's strongest differentiation is Tokyo/Japan founder perspective
4. **Rerun branded control and broad prompts** after any content changes to measure whether grounding and discovery improve
