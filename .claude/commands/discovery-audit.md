# /discovery-audit

Read `agents/discovery.md` and `playbooks/prompt-runs.md` first.

Gather one question at a time:
1. founder profile
2. audit scope: broad or topic-specific
3. target provider or comparison view

Produce a discovery diagnosis and save it to:
- `founders/<founder-id>/evidence/YYYY-MM-DD-discovery-audit.md`
or
- `outputs/comparisons/YYYY-MM-DD-discovery-audit-[founder-id].md`
